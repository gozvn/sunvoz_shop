Our Story

From a small seed of an idea to a growing community of mindful homemakers — discover the journey that brought Sunvoz to life and the values that guide everything we do.

Our Mission

To make sustainable living effortless and beautiful. We curate and craft home essentials that replace single-use and harmful products with nature-inspired alternatives — without compromising on design, quality, or functionality.

Our Vision

A world where every home is a sanctuary of sustainability. We envision a future where choosing eco-friendly products is the norm, not the exception — where nature and modern living exist in perfect harmony.

What We Stand For

Three pillars that define everything we create

Sustainable Sourcing

Every material we use is carefully vetted for its environmental impact. From FSC-certified bamboo to organic cotton and recycled metals, we trace every ingredient back to its source to ensure ethical and sustainable practices throughout our supply chain.

Artisan Partnerships

We work directly with skilled artisans and small workshops around the world, ensuring fair wages and preserving traditional craftsmanship. Each product carries the soul and expertise of the hands that made it — connecting you to real people and real stories.

Zero Waste Promise

We're committed to eliminating waste at every stage — from production to packaging to delivery. All our packaging is 100% compostable or recyclable, and we actively offset our carbon footprint to achieve truly carbon-neutral operations.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the Sunvoz story

2020

Founded

Sunvoz was born from a simple idea: home products shouldn't harm the planet. Our founders started with a kitchen table and a dream.

2021

First Collection

Launched our inaugural Kitchen Essentials line — 25 products that quickly became customer favorites and set the standard for what Sunvoz would become.

2022

10,000 Customers

Reached our first major milestone of 10,000 happy customers. Expanded into Bath & Body and Living Room collections.

2023

Carbon Neutral

Achieved full carbon neutrality across all operations — from manufacturing to shipping. Partnered with global reforestation programs.

2024

International Expansion

Began shipping to 25+ countries worldwide. Opened partnerships with artisan communities across Southeast Asia and South America.

2025

Community Program

Launched the Sunvoz Community Program — empowering local communities through educational workshops and sustainable living initiatives.
